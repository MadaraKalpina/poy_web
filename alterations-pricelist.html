Alterations Price List

I’ll need to see the garment before I can confirm the exact price; in some cases, a photo will suffice. If size/length alterations are required, you will, of course, need to try the garment on.

I’m based in Prague 6, but we can also arrange a courier service.

I usually complete most jobs within 1–3 weeks, depending on the complexity and my current workload; there’s a 50% surcharge for express repairs or alterations.
